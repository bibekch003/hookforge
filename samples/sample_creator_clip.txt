I spent three years editing my own videos and losing the first thousand viewers in the first three seconds. Then I stopped starting with the intro. Now I open with the result, the mess, or the mistake. Here is the workflow I actually use now. I pull the loudest thirty seconds out of the raw take and I build the hook around that moment. Then I render the same hook in three aspect ratios so I can post it everywhere without re-editing. The captions get rewritten for each platform because a forty two character line breaks on a reel. And the thumbnail is the last thing I make, not the first. That single change doubled my watch time in six weeks.
